Privacy & Terms

How GNPPAC states it uses and protects information provided by supporters.

Privacy Policy / Terms of Service

GNPPAC states that data is used only for political education, advocacy, and action. Personal information is to be securely stored and used to improve participation in educational and action-oriented initiatives.

The organization states it will not share information with third parties without explicit consent, except when required by law. Supporters may opt out of communications at any time.

Production launch should add data-retention details, cookie disclosures, contact instructions, and the effective date after legal review.
