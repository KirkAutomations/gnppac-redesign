Founding Fathers

A core of committed supporters helping establish the foundation for GNPPAC’s statewide work.

The commitment

Put your name behind the cause.

GNPPAC describes a Founding Father as a stakeholder willing to put time and treasure toward the renewal of freedom in Michigan.

The program seeks at least 100 Founding Fathers to establish a firm operating foundation for delegate protection, activist coordination, issue education, reporting, candidate endorsements, and support.
