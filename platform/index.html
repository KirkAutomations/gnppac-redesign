The Pro-Michigan Platform

A governing agenda for freedom, accountability, security, and the renewal of Michigan.

Twelve priorities

Michigan government should serve Michigan people.

This page condenses GNPPAC's published platform. The original positions remain available at gnppac.us.

01

Curb spending and taxation

Cut spending and reduce taxes so Michiganders can keep more of what they earn.

02

Curtail illegal immigration

End sanctuary policies and coordinate against trafficking, illegal drugs, and national-security threats.

03

Nullify unlawful federal acts

Refuse state cooperation with federal actions that violate constitutional limits.

04

Reduce unelected bureaucratic power

Restore legislative responsibility and remove needless barriers to work and enterprise.

05

Increase transparency and accountability

Extend meaningful public-record access and make government decisions easier to inspect.

06

Affirm election integrity

Strengthen transparency, chain of custody, voter-roll maintenance, audits, and public confidence.

07

End corporate welfare

Stop pay-to-play subsidies and restrict the revolving door between elected office and lobbying.

08

Establish sound monetary policy

Protect the right to hold precious metals and cryptocurrency without undue restrictions.

09

Secure unalienable rights

Reject emergency rule that treats constitutional rights as temporary permissions.

10

Respect and empower law enforcement

Protect communities while reforming laws that needlessly pit citizens and officers against each other.

11

Protect children and parental rights

Defend life, parental authority, and the dignity and safety of children.

12

Ensure energy independence

Support abundant, reliable energy and innovation instead of politically imposed scarcity.

Content is a design condensation of GNPPAC's public platform. No policy positions have been added.
