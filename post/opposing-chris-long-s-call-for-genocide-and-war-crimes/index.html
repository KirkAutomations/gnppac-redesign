Opposing Chris Long's Call for Genocide and War Crimes

GNPPAC calls for responsible, America First leadership and rejects rhetoric advocating indiscriminate violence against civilians.

GNPPAC argues that party officers must meet a basic standard of judgment and moral seriousness, especially when discussing war and the lives of civilians.

The statement distinguishes provocative negotiating language from public advocacy of mass killing and warns that reckless rhetoric creates resentment, blowback, and consequences borne by young Americans.

The organization calls on Michigan Republicans to repudiate the comments and remove Chris Long as Grassroots Vice Chair.

Political accountability requires public evidence, clear standards, and the courage to state a position directly.
